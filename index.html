HOW IT WORKS

A straightforward study routine.

↑

1. Upload

Use a three-column question bank: number, question, and correct answer. Review and fix extracted rows if needed.

⇄

2. Generate

Pick question numbers. We select the correct answer plus two distinct, randomly chosen answers from your bank.

▣

3. Study

Choose MCQ or Classic mode, flip to reveal, and navigate with keyboard or on-screen controls.

↓

4. Take it with you

Save interactive HTML, an offline ZIP, PDF via print dialog, CSV, or Excel. Review HTML decks offline.

PRIVACY FIRST

Your questions. Your device.

The website does not send uploaded question banks to our servers. Parsing and deck generation happen in your browser. No login, account, database, or saved study sessions in this version. Only DOCX files can be uploaded; printable PDF export is available.
